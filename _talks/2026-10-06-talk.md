Presented a talk and poster at the 2026 annual meeting of Stanford Centaur, the Center for Automated Reasoning. I presented SimSpect, my work on formally specifying the security guarantees of hardware Spectre defenses and automatically generating and executing litmus tests that check simulated defense prototypes against those guarantees.
