Presented a talk and poster at YArch, the Young Architects Workshop held with ISCA 2026, a venue for early-stage graduate students in computer architecture to share work in progress and get feedback. I presented SimSpect, my work on formally specifying the security guarantees of hardware Spectre defenses and automatically generating and executing litmus tests that check simulated defense prototypes against those guarantees.
